Who we are

Conviction over consensus.

We are former founders and operators who know how hard the first miles are. Besago leads seed and Series A rounds with checks from $500K to $8M — and we stay in the trenches long after the wire clears.

Our name comes from an old word meaning to be equal to the task. We look for teams who are equal to theirs: relentless, clear-eyed, and obsessed with the problem they're solving.

  • First check or lead — we decide fast, in days not months
  • Hands-on support: hiring, GTM, fundraising strategy
  • Follow-on reserves for every position we take
